    Lab 2 · Diffraction 繞射

    Why do chipmakers need shorter light?

    Light is a wave. When it squeezes through an opening about as small as its own wavelength, it fans out and the print turns blurry. Shrink the wavelength and watch the spot on the right become sharp again.

    On a real mask the openings are drawn 4× bigger, then the lens shrinks them.

    Think of a marker

    Writing tiny letters with a fat marker gives blobs. The wavelength is the size of the marker tip. EUV light is a tip about 14 times finer than ArF.

    Lab 3 · The Rayleigh equation

    How small can this machine print?

    Three knobs decide the smallest line: the wavelength λ, the lens opening NA, and the process factor k₁. Turn them and see if the machine can still print the pattern you want.

    Above 1.0 is only possible with water between lens and wafer (immersion).

    0.25 is the physical floor for one exposure.

    Why it matters

    Modern EUV scanners match layers to about 1 to 1.5 nm, only a few atoms. Miss by more and the wires of one floor do not touch the posts of the next, so the chip fails.
